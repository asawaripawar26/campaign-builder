      How the generator works
      1. Read and recalculate. Tabs are read by header name. Names and daily budgets are rebuilt here: Google daily = monthly ÷ 30.4 (Google's monthly cap), Meta daily = monthly ÷ days in month.
      2. Concepts are the hub. Every ad, test variant and PMax asset group points at a Concept ID. The concept supplies the landing page and CTA, and its status and claims decide whether it may export.
      3. Claims and status gates. A concept that makes a claim exports only when its claim is Approved with a source, an approver and a date. Test ad sets accept "Ready for testing" or "Testing" concepts. Retired never exports.
      4. Creative mapping. Files come from Creative_Library by concept, execution (version) and shape: 4:5 (else 1:1) for Meta Feed, 1.91:1, 1:1 and 4:5 images, logos and videos for PMax. A library change affects the next export only.
      5. Variant matrix. For each test concept, every Hook × every Headline becomes an ad named CONCEPT_execution_hook_headline. Evidence is estimated per concept, per hook and per ad, so nobody mistakes 3 trials for a verdict.
      6. PMax in place. The Editor export proves the campaign exists exactly once, that the asset groups to pause exist, and that new names don't collide. Campaign rows carry only the name and budget: blank fields mean "no change" to Editor, so bidding, targeting and tracking settings are left alone. Column names are matched to your export's own spelling.
      7. Draft export leaves out rows with {{placeholders}} or unapproved claims, and lets TEST files through, for a dry-run import. Never launch a draft.
